# Principles: Leila Hormozi

## People decide the outcome

**1. Execution, not strategy, is where most businesses fail, and execution is people.**
- "Most businesses don't fail because of a bad strategy. They fail because they have poor execution." (S016)
- "If you have better Talent you will beat your competitors, Talent beat strategy." (S048)
- Sources: 6 (S013, S016, S022, S026, S048, S061). Confidence: high. She says of the portfolio companies she reviewed, about 2 were capped by strategy and the rest by execution (S016).

**2. Hiring is the founder's main job, and you are one hire away from the growth you want.**
- "your number one job as a leader is to find the people to help you build your business" (S022)
- "we've always been really one hire away from all the growth we wanted all the leverage we needed" (S026)
- Sources: 8 (S016, S019, S022, S026, S030, S040, S048, S061). Confidence: high.

**3. Values come before skill, experience, and pedigree.**
- "That always has to come ahead of experience and pedigree." (S047)
- "soft skills and character is much harder to teach than hard skills" (S030)
- Sources: 8 (S009, S012, S016, S030, S047, S050, S060, S062). Confidence: high.

**4. Hire for skill, test it, and do not trust the interview performance.**
- "You don't hire people for experience you hire people for skill experience is only a proxy to skill" (S030)
- "The interview is the best you're gonna get from this person." (S063)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S011, S030, S040, S047, S063, S022). Confidence: high.

**5. If a candidate has no other options, do not hire them.**
- "if somebody doesn't have another option you should not hire them" (S026)
- Sources: 2 (S022, S026). Confidence: medium.

**6. A bad employee is usually the leader's fault first: hiring, training, or role.**
- "What if I told you that that person was actually your fault that they acted that way, why, because you hired and trained them." (S049)
- "I do not believe people are stupid, I believe that most people are just bad teachers." (S051)
- Sources: 8 (S010, S031, S036, S042, S049, S051, S041, S061). Confidence: high.

## Culture is what you do

**7. The founder is the culture, and the team mirrors the founder.**
- "Guess who makes the culture? You are the culture." (S016)
- "This is like the hardest thing to see, but your company is really a mirror of you." (S060)
- Sources: 9 (S001, S016, S040, S047, S051, S054, S056, S060, S065). Confidence: high.

**8. Culture is what you tolerate and enforce, especially in who comes in and who goes out.**
- "Culture is not what you say, it's what you tolerate, it's what you enforce." (S060)
- "words mean nothing behavior is it King and so how we treat people uh or how we respond to people when they behave a certain way is how you develop a culture" (S025)
- Sources: 7 (S012, S025, S041, S054, S060, S062, S009). Confidence: high.

**9. Praise over punishment; consequences are facts, not threats.**
- "I want to get there in a way that builds a culture based off praise not punishment." (S010)

- Sources: 8 (S007, S010, S012, S019, S025, S044, S050, S054). Confidence: high.

**10. Transparency and context make better decisions and safer teams.**
- "The person with the most context makes the best decisions." (S053)
- "When people feel safe they do their best work because they're not in fear." (S057)
- Sources: 7 (S018, S020, S025, S049, S053, S057, S043). Confidence: high.

## Hold the standard, kindly

**11. Be kind, not nice. Nice protects your comfort and costs them their growth.**
- "Being nice can actually be really cruel." (S058)
- "My job is not to be liked. My job is to be a great coach." (S016)
- Sources: 6 (S013, S016, S041, S056, S058, S060). Confidence: high.

**12. Have the hard conversation now; avoiding it is selfish and creates long-term harm.**
- "If you avoid giving feedback to your team it makes you selfish it doesn't make you a leader." (S056)
- "if you avoid the conversation you create long-term dysfunction whether it's in the role in the company across departments" (S032)
- Sources: 7 (S016, S031, S032, S036, S041, S056, S058). Confidence: high.

**13. Tell people what to do instead, tied to the goal they told you they have.**

- "A lot of times what we do is we anchor to the thing that we don't want rather than anchoring to the thing that they do want." (S056)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S012, S018, S051, S054, S056, S065). Confidence: high.

**14. The CEO holds everyone accountable, executives most of all.**

- "we over fire and over discipline front line and we under fire and under discipline management" (S041)
- Sources: 5 (S028, S031, S041, S042, S053). Confidence: high.

**15. Delegate and follow up; delegation is a ladder, not a switch.**
- "I can't just delegate and forget about it, I have to delegate and follow up." (S053)
- "it made me very strong and everyone around me very weak codling your team robs them of the opportunity to develop those skills themselves" (S017)
- Sources: 7 (S011, S012, S017, S035, S053, S060, S021). Confidence: high.

## Founder role

**16. Build a company that runs without you; a business that relies on you is worth little.**
- "not to mention making a business that relies on you there's no value in that type of business" (S017)
- "a business cannot survive off one brain" (S022)
- Sources: 7 (S017, S021, S022, S026, S048, S060, S011). Confidence: high.

**17. As you grow, your job moves from control to letting go, and from doing to deciding.**
- "As you grow, it's all about letting go, which is just like complete opposite of the kind of person that you become." (S060)
- "I believe that me making good decisions is the best use of my time." (S062)
- Sources: 6 (S011, S021, S027, S048, S060, S062). Confidence: high.

**18. Become the talent you want to attract. Top people will not work for a mediocre leader.**
- "When I think of how do I attract better talent I think of how do I become better talent." (S003)
- "Great people won't work for a mediocre leader and so the better you are the better Talent you will attract." (S051)
- Sources: 5 (S003, S010, S023, S030, S051). Confidence: high.

**19. Stay hands-on enough to know what good looks like.**
- "i try for the first year year and a half of the business to be the primary operator before i hand off to someone" (S028)
- "Like, I have 150 people. Do not act like my company. Start talking to people on the phone. You are not too good for it." (S027)
- Sources: 5 (S027, S028, S053, S063, S052). Confidence: high. She defends being called hands-on and tactical (S053).

## Focus and growth

**20. Do the right thing at the right time in the right order; stage beats tactic.**
- "The roles change as the business grows and so your expectation of what it looks like in one level is not what it looks like in the next." (S047)
- "The difference between a $50,000 month and a $300,000 month is not working harder. It's actually just knowing what to stop doing and then knowing what to do." (S027)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S027, S028, S047, S060). Confidence: high.

**21. Focus. Say no to good opportunities that are not yours.**
- "Even if it's a great opportunity, it's not our opportunity." (S009)
- "I Relish in saying no because it has served me so many times in my life" (S024)
- Sources: 7 (S004, S009, S013, S018, S021, S024, S027). Confidence: high.

**22. Fix the one constraint with the highest return; do not move seven things an inch.**

- "a perfect business likely makes no profit and doesn't grow" (S020)
- Sources: 5 (S013, S019, S020, S052, S057). Confidence: high.

**23. Do not solve problems with money alone; build capacity first.**
- "if you solve problems with just money, the company's basically built on stilts" (S009)
- "You have to put inventory before the bottleneck." (S016)
- Sources: 3 (S009, S016, S053). Confidence: high.

## Decisions and self-management
