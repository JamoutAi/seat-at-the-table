# Books: Leila Hormozi

Built only from what she says in public about books (talks, interviews, and her own channel in the corpus). No book text is reproduced. As of late 2026 she has not published a book of her own; her frameworks come from her videos, workshops, and the Acquisition.com scaling material she built with Alex.

## Her own book (in progress)

- **Status:** A leadership book. In early 2026 she said a first draft was done, built around a 12-step flywheel, targeted for Q1 2027 and likely self-published (S011). Later in 2026 she said it was postponed for personal reasons (S021). An audience member referred to it as "$100M Leadership" (S052); she has not confirmed a title in the corpus. An earlier idea was a book on following the plan regardless of mood (S011), and she says the writing process was hard for her because it is solo work (S004).
- **Core thesis, as she describes it:** a cycle of learning to lead yourself, lead others, lead leaders, and create leaders who create a culture that creates leaders. Three outcomes: a person who has never led becomes a good teammate, a small-team leader gains the confidence to lead a big team, and a big-team leader learns to develop leaders (S011).
- **Quote:** "So it's really like a cycle of learning how to lead yourself, learning how to lead others, learning how to lead leaders, and then learning how to create leaders." (S011)
- **Where its ideas show up already:** frameworks.md #12 to #17 and #22 (accountability, feedback, praise over punishment, delegation, firing). She says stories she tells in interviews are going into it (S016).

## The Acquisition.com scaling road map and workshops

- **What it is:** The 10 levels of business and the Value Acceleration Method she teaches with the Acquisition.com team; she presents it in S027 and in keynotes (S022, S026). Portfolio results she cites: about 1.8x revenue and 3x profit in year one, about 2.3x revenue and 4.7x profit in year two (S022). Treat these as her claims, not audited figures.
- **Taught in:** S022, S026, S027, S018, S019, S020, S052, S055 (workshop Q&As).

## Alex Hormozi's books

- She calls them, with a caller, "the business bibles" (S021) and uses his story structure as a model for her own writing (S011). She ran his book launches operationally (S005, S010). His books belong to the Hormozi seat; this seat does not teach them.

## Books she credits

- **John Wooden on the Pyramid of Success:** the source of her company value "competitive greatness" and of her CEO self-rating exercise, run with about 900 CEOs (S023, S005).
- **Ben Horowitz, The Hard Thing About Hard Things:** helped her after the 2019 layoff; source of peacetime and wartime CEO and "bad versus worse" decisions (S023, S016, S061).
- **Howard Schultz, Pour Your Heart Into It:** she calls him an idol; used for the Starbucks turnaround example (S023, S014).
- **Claire Hughes Johnson, Scaling People:** her pick as the modern High Output Management (S010).
- **Patrick Lencioni and Jim Collins:** she says she has read all of both and prefers research-based work to gurus (S052).
- **Matt Mochary on being a CEO:** recommended; she says he declined to mentor her years earlier (S052).
- **Albert Ellis and Steven Hayes:** the psychology behind how she handles anxiety and thoughts; she rereads her psychology books yearly (S005, S003, S004).
- **John Mackey:** win-win-win decisions (S012, S014).
- **Rich Dad Poor Dad:** work to learn, not to earn, when young (S009, S001).
- **High Output Management (Andy Grove):** her 2022 five-part management series borrows the name and idea of managing by output (S029, S042, S043, S044, S046).
- She also says she read six or seven books on meetings before redesigning hers (S046) and "like six very good books on business," with most of her learning from doing (S006).
