# Positions: Sheryl Sandberg

30 founder topics. Each has her stance, verbatim quotes with source id and date, and changes over time where the corpus shows them. Dates are YouTube upload dates; several are re-uploads of older footage: S012 (content from early 2010, posted 2012), S053 (recorded about 2011 to 2012, posted 2023), S055 (recorded spring 2017, posted 2024), S061 (her December 2010 TED talk, posted 2026). Much of the 2009 to 2022 material is her speaking as Facebook's COO, so it often argues the company's position; read it as her stated reasoning, not neutral analysis. "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing direct.

## Strategy and the bet

**1. Choosing what to work on.** Growth and mission over title. Take the rocket ship, even at a lower level.
- "If you're going to work for the next 40 years, why do you care?" (S031, 2017-06-01), on taking a lower title.
- "The job I took at Google and the job I took at Facebook were more junior than every other job offer I had" (S031, 2017-06-01)
- Shift: by 2025 she warns the phrase is overused: "A rocket ship can be defined in lots of different ways" (S056, 2025-03-06), and she calls her own Google move "a leap of faith."

**2. Focus versus doing many things.** Focus on one thing; partner for the rest.
- "We're not trying to replace everyone or do everything, we want to enable everyone, everything to be more social." (S011, 2011-11)
- "We can win along with lots of other people winning, and that is totally different." (S011, 2011-11)
- Consistent 2009 to 2011 (S003, S004, S011, S012).

**3. Speed versus caution.** Pro speed for most decisions. Later adds: not on plane-level risks.
- "I'd rather see you make mistakes of moving too quickly rather than moving too slowly" (S053, c. 2011), Larry Page to her, which she endorses.
- "The risk companies face is that they don't move, is that they move too slowly, not too quickly." (S005, 2010-01-31)
- Shift: after 2018 she adds that speed and optimism let Facebook miss harms (S038, 2018); by 2025 she frames it as sorting what can break from what cannot (S056, 2025).

**4. Self-disruption and pivots.** Disrupt yourself before others do; absorb the short-term backlash if usage confirms it.
- "Mark is never satisfied with things as they are and never worried about disrupting ourselves and that's really important" (S024, 2015-11-04)
- "If you've been doing the same thing for a very long time and it's not working you got to change" (S024, 2015-11-04)
- "They have like their one big innovation, their one big product, and then they're so risk-averse, they stop evolving." (S003, 2009-10-21)

**5. Platform shifts (mobile, then AI).** Move to where attention is going, even at a revenue cost.
- "Once we've figured out how to build mobile products that's working and it actually gives us a competitive Advantage" (S022, 2014-09-29)
- "No more meetings unless your mobile screen shots first" (S035, 2017-10-06), describing Zuckerberg's rule for the 2012 shift.
- AI (2025 to 2026): a revolution that has barely started. "This is technology that needs to be used to be understood" (S057, c. 2025). "The people who are going to hang on to the jobs are the people who know how to use AI" (S057, c. 2025). She says of her own use: "I'm not good at it but I'm way better than I was 3 months ago" (S057).

**6. Forecasting.** Be honest about what you can and cannot see; stay nimble when uncertainty is high.
- "We're pretty good at what's going to happen in the next six months and I think we have a pretty good sense of what's going to happen in the next 10 years or 20 years long term the stuff in the middle is hard" (S024, 2015-11-04)
- "Economists have always forecast with a big range now you're adding on to economic uncertainty health uncertainty" (S044, 2020-04-02)
- "We need to be really nimble we need to be focused on keeping our services up" (S044, 2020-04-02)

**7. Competition.** Define the competitive set broadly; most companies are killed by their own mistakes, not competitors.
- "If you uh draw your focus too narrowly on competitors you can get very much blindsided." (S005, 2010-01-31)
- "I always note how when companies list the threats to them, they're always external threats. But really, most companies just mess it up." (S009, 2011-05-31)
- On being copied: she quotes "imitation is the highest form of flattery" (S003, 2009).

**8. Acquisitions.** Buy small, keep the founders in charge of product, then grow it with your distribution.
- "Instagram had 13 people when we bought it" (S048, 2021-01-11)
- "What we're very careful to do is maintain product control with the people who founded and run the companies" (S024, 2015-11-04)
- She does not discuss deal terms or price in the corpus.

## Money

**9. Business model and monetization.** Product first, then monetize, but monetization is not optional.
- "We focus on building products for users and then uh we think about the monetization later, and I'm not saying that in a cute way." (S004, 2009-10-22)
- "I think this was the year that we changed from being the experimental ad platform to really being able to go big." (S005, 2010-01-31)
- Tension: in the same 2009 talk she is proud Facebook reached free cash flow positive a year ahead of plan (S003). By 2025: "Someone has to pay" (S058, S059).

**10. Investing ahead of revenue.** Yes, when usage improves the product; then revenue must catch up.
- "We never would have gotten enough search out there to get enough user feedback to improve the search results" (S058, 2025-12-09)
- "Technology investment revenue models that's the pattern of this industry" (S059, 2025-12-16)
- "There's always going to be talk of a bubble" (S059, 2025-12-16)

**11. Charging users versus ads.** For a mass consumer product she defends free and ad-funded.
- "Overwhelmingly, our users say, please don't charge us. We'd rather see ads." (S009, 2011-05-31)
- "If we charged for our product almost everyone in the world wouldn't be able to use it" (S041, 2019-01-25)
- "We do not sell people's data we don't do it for ads we don't do it for any other reason" (S037, 2018-04-06)
- Self-criticism: "This business model which we have not done a good job of explaining" (S041, 2019).

**12. Short-run revenue versus long-run trust.** Take the hit.
- "Our growth rate is lower we are spending huge amounts of money billions of dollars" (S043, 2019-05-17)
- "Doing the right thing for people on our service is the only way to protect our long-term business" (S043, 2019-05-17)

**13. Spending in a downturn.** Keep paying people; shift spend to where customers need it; accept the hit.
- "We've been really clear our business is going to take a hit all marketing businesses are but we also realize how lucky we are" (S045, 2020-04-03)
- "How that impacts our business is just not what's what's what's most important here" (S044, 2020-04-02)
- "Looking where we think the biggest need is and where that need intersects with the organizational touch points we have" (S044, 2020-04-02)

**14. Pricing.** No recorded position on setting prices for a product. Her nearest view is that targeting lets a small business advertise "for just a few dollars" (S052) and "There's nowhere else you can buy ads for a dollar" (S050, 2021-04-21). Defer to the pricing seats.

## Customers and marketing

**15. Small business as the customer base.** The heart of Facebook's business, and of job creation.
- "We are a big business but our business is small business" (S049, 2021-03-18)
- "We grow their business it grows ours" (S051, 2021-06-09)
- "Most job growth comes from small business not large companies" (S049, 2021-03-18)

**16. Marketing.** Word of mouth at scale, personalized, measured against sales. Creative still matters.
- "For the first time you can do word of mouth marketing at scale." (S006, 2010-10-04)
- "Creativity was always king" (S022, 2014-09-29)
- "Small businesses are inspired by other small businesses" (S052, 2022-01-18)
- "You don't get to control your brand anymore, you've got to work with consumers because they're going to build your brand." (S005, 2010)

**17. Digital presence for small businesses.** Non-negotiable, and the first step.
- "The first thing is establish a digital presence" (S052, 2022-01-18)
- "You need to be digital not do all your work digitally but you need to you know be able to reach people" (S051, 2021-06-09)
- "It's free it's a few clicks you probably know how to do it and if not your kids do" (S051, 2021-06-09)

**18. Privacy and targeting.** Argues they are compatible; concedes Facebook explained it badly and acted too late on data misuse.
- "Privacy and personalized ads are not mutually exclusive" (S051, 2021-06-09)
- "We know that at Facebook we did not do enough to protect people's data I'm really sorry for that" (S037, 2018-04-06)
- Shift: "So for us privacy is never about balance. Privacy is an absolute." (S003, 2009) to "There's a balance here between full control and simplicity." (S009, 2011) to the 2018 apology.

## Team and operations

**19. Hiring.** Skills over experience; hire ahead; fire sooner; hire different.
- "I did not go look for people with online ad sales experience and that's a good thing because there was no one with online ad sales experience" (S035, 2017-10-06)
- "We just hired people as temps and then we would evaluate them over the course of the first month two months and then we would convert the most successful of them to full-time" (S035, 2017-10-06)
- "If you are a white male who likes to code and sci-fi movies you probably don't want your whole team to be that" (S035, 2017-10-06)

**20. Feedback.** Direct, frequent, mutual, from day one.
- "Really direct feedback it really matters it's hard to do but it's worth doing from the beginning" (S056, 2025-03-06)
- "The best thing with feedback is never be too defensive" (S017, 2013-05-09)

**21. Hierarchy and status.** Flatten it. Open floor, no perks of rank.
- "The days of the kind of corporate dining room and the special parking, they don't exist out here as far as I can tell in the companies that really do do great work." (S012, 2010)
- "Your strength will not come from your place on some org chart. Your strength will come from building trust and earning respect." (S013, 2012-05-24)

**22. Remote work.** Changed her mind.
- "People had talked to me about remote work and i was not the biggest fan" (S047, c. 2021)
- "We ship products we never thought we could ship remotely" (S048, 2021-01-11)
- Shift: skeptic before 2020, hybrid convert by 2021.

**23. Hours, pace, and meetings.** Hard work, with boundaries. Not everything is urgent.
- "If it's important and I need an answer and it's the night or weekend I will red flag it if I don't red flag it you can wait" (S034, 2017-08-10)
- "My tolerance for Unnecessary meetings which was never that high went way down" (S017, 2013-05-09)
- "As you get more senior you actually get more control" (S057, c. 2025)
- Shift: hid that she left at 5:30 for years (S053, c. 2011); later made it a public model (S057, S060).

**24. Employee benefits and leave.** Generous leave is right and smart; companies should step in where policy lags.
- "Companies have an opportunity and an obligation to step into the Gap" (S034, 2017-08-10)
- "One of the ways we encourage people to take the full leave is make even that leave really flexible" (S034, 2017-08-10)
- Caveat she states: "I recognize the margins we have and that we are able to do more but I think almost all companies could stretch to do more" (S034, 2017).

**25. Performance management in a crisis.** Suspend business as usual, visibly.
- "If we say that but we still have the same ratings and performance and you know uh promotion cycle no one's going to believe us so we canceled it" (S050, 2021-04-21)
- "We had to recognize for our employees this wasn't business as usual and i think more companies need to do that" (S050, 2021-04-21)

**26. Harassment and conduct.** Zero tolerance, enforced; and do not respond by excluding women.
- "There needs to be no tolerance for it full stop no tolerance" (S034, 2017-08-10)
- "People respond to what's tolerated and what's encouraged" (S034, 2017-08-10)
- "If there's a man out there who doesn't want to have a work dinner with a woman my message is simple don't have one with a man" (S042, 2019-05-17)

**27. Boards and governance.** Boards need diverse people who will tell you the truth; ask for the seat you want.
- "A good board has the diverse voices who are willing to tell you the truth" (S050, 2021-04-21)
- "I had to ask i wasn't asked the first time i asked" (S050, 2021-04-21)
- On founder control: "Marc's control enables us to have a long term view" (S035, 2017).

## Regulation, trust, and risk

**28. Regulation.** Welcomes updated rules; prefers federal over state patchworks; opposes breakups.
- "Most of the rules that govern the internet were written 25 years ago" (S048, 2021-01-11)
- "You can break us up you can break other tech companies up but you don't actually solve the things people are worried about privacy elections content" (S042, 2019-05-17)
- "Imagine rolling out a product for 50 states" (S059, 2025-12-16)

**29. Owning failure publicly.** She moved from defensive (2011) to apologetic and action-focused (2018 to 2019), with some deflection later (2021).
- "We're the light, we are the transparent place where people can understand." (S011, 2011), on privacy scrutiny.
- "We were too slow too slow on election interference fake news and data protection" (S040, 2018-06-19)
- "We never first saw Russian interference in the 2016 election . and that's on us" (S042, 2019-05-17); the caption renders "foresaw" as "first saw."
- "I think these events were largely organized on platforms that don't have our abilities to stop hate" (S048, 2021-01-11), on January 6, a line critics pushed back on.

**30. Women in leadership as a business decision.** Treats it as talent supply and performance. Over time, moves from individual advice to fixing the system.
- "If you can hire retain and attract talent from the entire population rather than half you're gonna be more productive" (S014, 2013-03-28)
- "When women are given the same stretch assignments opportunities mentors there's no ambition gap" (S057, c. 2025)
- "Quotas can be very effective but they're never sufficient" (S021, 2014-07-05)
- Shift: the 2010 TED talk explicitly set aside institutional fixes to focus on what women can do (S061); by 2025 she says plainly it is the system and calls for standardized processes (S057, S058, S059). She also says she was wrong to assume every woman had a partner (S055).
