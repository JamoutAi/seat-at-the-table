# Sources: Sheryl Sandberg

61 sources, all video transcripts (YouTube captions), about 397,986 words, 2009 to 2026. The mix: her Facebook-era business talks and on-stage interviews (TechCrunch at Davos 2009 and 2010, the Churchill Club with Charlene Li, Web 2.0 Summit, IAB keynotes, LSE, the Economic Club of Chicago, Dreamforce with Marc Benioff, Fortune with Marc Andreessen, Bloomberg Studio 1.0, Masters of Scale, ZEIT at Davos, CNBC, Reuters Next, the Detroit Economic Club, the Executives' Club of Chicago, Tampa Bay and Dubai chambers, EDCUtah, and the US Conference of Mayors); commencement and class day speeches (Barnard 2011, UCLA Anderson 2011, Harvard Business School 2012, UC Berkeley 2016, MIT 2018) and the Naval Academy's Forrestal Lecture; the Lean In book tour (Stanford's Clayman Institute, the Computer History Museum with Eric Schmidt, the HBS W50 Summit, the Pentagon, TED 2010 and 2013); the Option B book tour with Adam Grant (Politics and Prose, the 92nd Street Y, Knowledge at Wharton, Chicago Humanities, and Oprah's Super Soul Sunday); Stanford GSB's View From The Top; and her post-Meta interviews (Glue Guys podcast 2025, Forbes, Bloomberg, CNBC, and She Said/She Said, 2025 to 2026), plus the All-In podcast (2024).

Exclusions and caveats: duplicate uploads were dropped (a CBS copy of the 2016 Berkeley speech, a second UCLA Anderson upload, the 2023 re-release of the Masters of Scale episode, and a second copy of the 2015 Pentagon talk). Six uploads were dropped because she is not the main speaker: two where she interviews someone else (Adam Grant on "Originals," and Arianna Huffington on "Thrive"), a Cornell webinar about Lean In in which she never speaks, a Churchill Club event where she interviews Chelsea Clinton, a Meta conference fireside where she interviews physicist Deborah Berebichez, and a New York Times Book Review podcast where her segment is about 15% of the episode. A Commonwealth Club Option B talk could not be fetched (captions disabled). Shared stages, where she has roughly 30 to 60% of the words: S011 (Charlie Rose with Mark Zuckerberg, who speaks most; only lines attributable to her are used), S018 (Marc Benioff), S022 (Katie Couric), S024 (Marc Andreessen and a Fortune moderator), S027 (Lori Goler), S028, S029, S030, S033 (Option B events with Adam Grant and a moderator), S035 (Masters of Scale, narrated mostly by Reid Hoffman with her clips), S040 (two mayors), S045 (Kevin O'Leary), and S056 (Glue Guys hosts). S054 (All-In) contains her interview as the first part of the episode; the rest is unrelated and has no business content from her. Several uploads are re-posts of older footage: S012 (content from early 2010, posted 2012), S053 (recorded about 2011 to 2012, posted 2023), S055 (Super Soul Sunday, recorded spring 2017, posted 2024), and S061 (her December 2010 TEDWomen talk, posted 2026). S040's date may not match the event. Undated uploads are given approximate dates ("c.") from content clues.

Dates are YouTube publish dates unless marked. Quotes come from captions, so punctuation was added during extraction and caption mis-hearings ("Cheryl," "heir on the side," "first saw" for "foresaw," "Marc's" for "Mark's") are kept as captured. No book text is used: the books are covered only through her own public teaching of them.
