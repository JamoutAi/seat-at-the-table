# Principles: Sheryl Sandberg

26 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from auto captions. Where a rule came from someone else (Eric Schmidt, Larry Page, Bill Campbell, Dave Goldberg, Adam Grant), it is noted.

## Choosing the bet

**1. Bet on growth and mission, not on title or position. Fast growth fixes most problems; slow growth breeds politics.**
- "When companies are growing quickly, and they're having a lot of impact, careers take care of themselves." (S013)
- "And when companies aren't growing quickly or the missions don't matter as much, that's when stagnation and politics come in." (S013)
- "You're always better off taking more Junior position in a company that's growing quickly and a more senior one in the slowing growing company" (S053)
- Sources: 6 (S008, S013, S017, S031, S053, S056). Confidence: high. Origin: Eric Schmidt's advice in 2001.

**2. Take the unglamorous job at the core of the future business.**
- "He said that's a tractor job and you're a Porsche" (S053), Omid Kordestani on the four-person self-serve AdWords team.
- "I said no I'm happy to be a tractor" (S053)
- "I think it's really important to sometimes do the things that are less than obvious" (S053)
- "Do real work. Take a sales quota, a line role, an ops job. Don't plan too much, and don't expect a direct climb." (S013)
- Sources: 4 (S013, S031, S053, S056). Confidence: high.

**3. Prioritize by saying no to good ideas, not only bad ones.**
- "Because they might be number three, but as a company, we can only do one and two." (S012)
- "We are focused on doing one thing incredibly well, we only really want to do one thing." (S011)
- Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S011, S012). Confidence: high.

**4. The bigger risk is moving too slowly. Make the mistakes of speed, not of caution.**
- "The risk companies face is that they don't move, is that they move too slowly, not too quickly." (S005)
- "As long as the mistakes you make are those of moving too fast, doing too much, and not necessarily having all the controls, great." (S012), relaying Larry Page after her $1M mistake at Google.
- "I think I would still like to see us move faster." (S005), naming her biggest regret of her first two years.
- Sources: 5 (S003, S005, S009, S012, S053). Confidence: high. Caveat: she later says the same speed bias let Facebook miss harms (rule 20).

**5. A bet needs a payoff. Courage is not the same as recklessness.**
- "You take risks for reward" (S031)
- "No risk is worth taking if there's no payoff" (S056)
- Sources: 3 (S031, S038, S056). Confidence: high.

**6. Don't draw a straight line. Hold a long-run dream and an 18-month plan, and do not connect them.**
- "Do not try to connect the short-term plan to the long run dream because you will miss opportunities because Facebook didn't exist" (S030)
- "If you try to map out where you are right now all the way to the end you will wind up on a very limited career path" (S036)
- "If I had mapped out career when I was sitting where you are, I would have missed my career." (S013)
- Sources: 5 (S013, S030, S031, S036, S046). Confidence: high.

## Money and the long run

**7. It can be right to invest ahead of revenue, if usage makes the product better. But someone has to pay.**
- "I do think there's times when it really makes sense to invest ahead of revenue in business models" (S058)
- "Over time the revenue is going to have to cover the costs" (S058)
- "You can have subscriptions you can have data services you can have ads those are the options" (S059)
- Sources: 3 (S053, S058, S059). Confidence: high.

**8. Run the company for the long run, and say out loud what the short run costs.**
- "We've always run the company for the long run and we do that by making the right decisions and the short run" (S043)
- "It may harm our revenue growth in the short run" (S043)
- Sources: 4 (S024, S035, S043, S045). Confidence: high.

**9. Doing right by people and doing well are usually the same decision.**
- "This isn't a tradeoff between the right thing to do and the smart thing to do this is both" (S034)
- "It's the smart thing to do. When you treat your employees, your contractors, the people that work to fulfill your mission well, they become more dedicated to you." (S027)
- "Why because it's the right thing to do but also because it pays off" (S039)
- Sources: 5 (S027, S032, S034, S039, S043). Confidence: high. Caveat: she admits Facebook's margins made this easier ("I recognize the margins we have," S034).

## Scaling the operation

**10. Sort plane from baggage. No shortcuts where failure breaks trust; ship imperfect where it is fixable.**
- "Some stuff is catering in baggage it doesn't matter that much you can take risk" (S056)
- "You're never going to get a product out the door cuz you're going to check it 8,000 times" (S056)
- Sources: 1 directly (S056), supported by the speed rules in S012 and S053. Confidence: medium.

**11. Build the systems for the size you will be, now.**
- "This promise I had made to make them feel good about scaling I took away in a week" (S035)
- "Company's a big dog like big company like you have to have systems in place" (S056)
- Sources: 3 (S009, S035, S056). Confidence: high.

**12. Hire for skills, hire ahead, hire different, and fire sooner. Hiring is the highest-leverage thing a leader does.**
- "The most important thing I do is, I hire really well." (S031)
- "You really do want to hire people who are better than you are and who are different than you are" (S035)
- "Our industry changes so quickly that almost none of us have done anything we do now before" (S017)
- Sources: 6 (S017, S031, S035, S050, S056, S013). Confidence: high.

**13. Measure, but measure the whole system. Clear metrics make people optimize the piece they control.**
- "If you give a group of people or even myself a clear metric-based target, they will run towards it almost without seeing anything else." (S012)
- "Equity compensation is everyone Rises or Falls" (S056)
- Sources: 2 (S012, S056). Confidence: medium.

**14. Small teams keep a big company fast.**
- "Keep people in small teams." (S012)
- "It's shown us that small teams can still do things totally from scratch." (S003)
- Sources: 3 (S003, S009, S012). Confidence: high.

## Leading and deciding

**15. Agree on how you will disagree before you join or partner.**
- "What you want from mark is process agreement on how you will work things out" (S035), Dave Goldberg's advice.
- "We also give each other feedback every week. Every week, little things, big things." (S003)
- Sources: 3 (S003, S031, S035). Confidence: high.

**16. Your job as the leader is to pull the truth out. If nobody told you, that is on you.**
- "A good leader recognizes that most people won't feel comfortable challenging authority, so it falls upon authority to encourage them to question." (S013)
- "And then I realized that if they hadn't told me, that was my fault." (S013)
- "Remember that to get to the truth, honesty must actually be rewarded." (S010)
- Sources: 7 (S007, S009, S010, S013, S035, S053, S056). Confidence: high.

**17. Lead, don't run. Decide with the people who own the work.**
- "I really believe we lead best when we walk side by side with our colleagues." (S010)
- "Command and control leadership I decide you do what I want that's not inspiring" (S053)
- "People will do what they are supposed to do if they work for you that's not what you want you want to have an aligned mission" (S035)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S007, S010, S013, S035, S053). Confidence: high.

**18. Debrief wins and losses. Organizations that hide failure don't learn.**
- "It's organizations that hide things under the rug that don't create the resilience because they don't learn" (S035)
- "If you want people to innovate and take risk, you have to applaud the failures." (S012)
- Sources: 5 (S012, S027, S035, S056, S060). Confidence: high.

**19. Own the mistake fast and specifically, then prove it with action and money.**
- "It's not enough to be sorry what matters is the action we are taking" (S040)
- "When you own your mistakes, you can work harder to correct them and even harder to prevent the next ones." (S038)
- "When they don't own it they're not taking back power they're giving up power" (S060)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S009, S038, S040, S041, S043, S060). Confidence: high.

**20. Look around corners. Believing in the good can blind you to the bad.**
- "It's painful when you miss something, when you make the mistake of believing so much in the good you're seeing that you don't see the bad." (S038)
- "And it's not enough to have a good idea. You have to know when to stop a bad one." (S038)
- "We were really focused on that and we weren't focused enough on being proactive enough" (S037)
- Sources: 5 (S037, S038, S040, S042, S043). Confidence: high. This is the lesson she drew from Facebook's 2016 to 2018 failures; it tempers rule 4.

**21. Tolerate the disagreeable giver; watch out for the agreeable taker.**
- "People get very confused by how people act not what they're doing" (S056)
- "It's too easy to look at the way someone says it versus what they're saying" (S060)
- Sources: 2 (S056, S060). Confidence: medium. Credited to Adam Grant's giver and taker research.

**22. Results and empathy are not opposites. You can be hardcore and kind.**
- "I think that leaning in is not hardcore is a mistake" (S059)
- "These things are not at odds and they're also not particularly masculine or particularly feminine" (S059)
- "You can be completely hard-charging demand business results work efficiently" (S057)
- Sources: 3 (S034, S057, S059). Confidence: high.

**23. Diverse teams make better decisions. Treat it as performance, not charity.**
- "The less diverse a team it is, the worse its performance is, and the more confident it is that its performance is good." (S023)
- "Cognitive diversity leads to better products and the way you get cognitive diversity is you get diversity of backgrounds" (S050)
- "We are still sourcing from half the population" (S014)
- Sources: 9 (S014, S017, S023, S034, S035, S038, S047, S050, S056). Confidence: high.

## Customers and the market

**24. Go where the customers already are, and make creative for that place.**
- "It's easier to go to where people are then get them to come to you." (S006)
- "You have to create the video for the platform" (S032)
- Sources: 4 (S006, S012, S022, S032). Confidence: high.

**25. Listen to customers, ship what they ask for, and teach them to use it.**
- "That's what you call listening to your customers" (S049)
- "Giving these tools but not having anyone know how to use them obviously doesn't work" (S052)
- Sources: 6 (S045, S049, S050, S051, S052, S047). Confidence: high.

## Resilience

**26. Setbacks are not personal, pervasive, or permanent. Resilience is a muscle you build before you need it.**
- "This is the lesson that not everything that happens to us, happens because of us." (S025)
- "Resilience is a muscle you build it you build it in yourself you build it in other people don't ask me how much you have ask me how to build it" (S030)
- "Anytime there's a real challenge is not the time to retreat, but the time to move forward" (S027)
- Sources: 9 (S025, S027, S028, S030, S033, S044, S046, S051, S056). Confidence: high.
