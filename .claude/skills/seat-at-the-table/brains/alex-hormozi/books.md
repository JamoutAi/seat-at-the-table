# Books: Alex Hormozi

Built only from how he teaches the books in public (launch events, talks, and interviews in the corpus). No book text is reproduced. His own account is that Offers, Leads, and Money Models started as one manuscript in 2021 about lead generation and monetization structures (S020).

## $100M Offers (July 2021)

- **Year:** July 2021, per his own account (S113).
- **Core thesis:** The offer is the biggest lever in the business. Build one so valuable that saying no feels stupid, sell it to a starving crowd that can pay, and stop competing as a commodity on price.
- **Key frameworks:** Value Equation (dream outcome and likelihood up, time and effort down); Grand Slam Offer built from a problem list, a solution list, and trim and stack; the starving crowd market screen; pricing and the virtuous cycle of price; enhancers (scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, naming). See frameworks.md #1 to #7.
- **Taught in:** S001 (live offer demo), S002, S027, S043, S045, S114, S115, S117, S118 (applied to e-commerce), S120, S122, S123.
- **Figures he cites:** sold for 99 cents with no paid marketing (S120); about 467,000 copies by August 2023 (S112).

## $100M Leads (August 19, 2023)

- **Year:** Launched live on 2023-08-19 (S112).
- **Core thesis:** More leads give you more chances to get everything else right. There are only four ways to let strangers know about your stuff; master one before adding another, then get others (lead getters) to do it for you.
- **Key frameworks:** Core Four (warm outreach, cold outreach, content, paid ads); lead getters (customers, employees, agencies, affiliates); lead magnets (three types, four formats); Rule of 100; More, Better, New; give-to-ask ratio; clear, not clever calls to action. See frameworks.md #15 to #20.
- **Taught in:** S112 (launch), S116 (lead magnets), S119 (lead magnet chapter preview), S120 (affiliate guide), S033 (referrals), S066 (first five customers), S052 and S070 (ads and volume).
- **Figures he cites:** about 463,000 registrations for the launch (S113); about 250,000 copies in its first month (S088); "over a million copies" across Offers and Leads (S063, S070).

## $100M Money Models (August 16, 2025)

- **Year:** Launched live on 2025-08-16 (S113).
- **Core thesis:** Cash flow kills businesses. A deliberate sequence of offers lets a customer's first 30 days of gross profit pay for acquiring and serving them, so customers, not investors, finance growth.
- **Key frameworks:** The four offer types (attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity) and their named tactics; client-financed acquisition (30-day cash > 2x CAC + COGS); never discount, change terms; sell at greatest deprivation. See frameworks.md #9 to #11.
- **Taught in:** S113 (seven-hour live launch, all four parts), S020, S046, S089, S060; earlier pieces in S037 (2021, the 30-day rule), S011 (2020, downsell ladder), and S069 (step-downs).
- **Figures he cites:** $105M to $106M in sales in 72 hours and a Guinness record for fastest-selling nonfiction book (S021, S075, S101).

## Gym Launch Secrets (year not stated in corpus)

- **Core thesis:** His earlier, smaller book for gym owners. In the corpus it appears only as the book a photography studio owner applied, which led to a portfolio investment (S047, S057). Do not cite its contents.
