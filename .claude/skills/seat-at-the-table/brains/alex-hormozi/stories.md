# Stories: Alex Hormozi

18 stories and case studies he reuses, most-told first. Figures are as he tells them; they sometimes vary between tellings.

1. **Broke on the gym floor.** A partner drained the account and a processor froze launch money; he fell to about $1,000 and slept on his gym floor, then collected $60K to $110K within weeks by selling what he knew. Lesson: skills are income insurance, and low fixed costs keep you in the game. (S029, S037, S043, S049, S050, S059, S065, S081, S083, S105, S113, S124)

2. **Nine businesses to one.** He ran up to nine or ten businesses at once until Leila pointed out Gym Launch made the most money for the least time and told him to pick one. He sold or shut the rest in 90 days, and the business went to $1.5M, then $4.5M a month. Lesson: focus replaces ego with income. (S001, S008, S048, S062, S077, S108)

3. **The gym money model.** Rivals ran $21 trials and took about 60 days to recoup about $100 of CAC. He ran a $500 to $600 six-week challenge with win-your-money-back, supplements, and annual prepay, collecting about $1,000 in 30 days on about $50 of CAC, worth about $245 per lead versus $53 for rivals. Presales funded each build-out. Lesson: when 30-day cash beats 2x CAC plus COGS, you can outspend anyone. (S020, S026, S037, S046, S060, S089, S112, S113, S114, S124)

4. **Level 10 skill set, level 2 opportunity.** As the only brick-and-mortar owner in a $30K mastermind of online marketers, he showed 30:1 front-end numbers and was told to teach gym owners instead of running gyms. He named $6,000 to an owner hoping for a no and got a yes; that became Gym Launch, $26M in its first 12 months. Lesson: the vehicle sets the ceiling, and your proven system is worth more than you think. (S008, S021, S040, S043, S045, S057, S078)

5. **Paying to learn.** With $50K saved he joined a $10K gym-owner mastermind that fixed his model in a day, and paid an ads expert $750 an hour instead of an agency $3K to $5K a month. Lesson: pay the ignorance tax early; buying other people's mistakes is the only way to buy time. (S006, S026, S042, S053, S067, S069, S072, S083, S113, S114, S116)

6. **The two oopsies: Prestige Labs and the price cut.** At $26M a year he started a supplement company instead of taking Gym Launch to $40M, and cut the license price from $800 to $600 a week to lower churn for a buyer. Churn did not move and the cut cost about $5M a year in profit. Lesson: the distraction at the next level is bigger, and lowering price rarely fixes churn. (S001, S045, S053, S078, S123)

7. **300 flyers versus 5,000 a day.** His first gym sent 300 flyers, got one call, and he declared flyers dead. His mentor tested 5,000 and then ran 5,000 a day. Lesson: low volume looks like volatility; do the volume before judging a channel. (S042, S044, S051, S052)

8. **Interviewing the top 2% on churn.** He polled thousands of licensed gyms for owners under 3% monthly churn; about 20 qualified, and all did the same five to seven things. Churn fell from 11% to 6%, which made the company sellable. Lesson: the answer already exists among your best customers. (S018, S040, S112)

9. **Cold outbound and the executive intervention.** His executives called a new outbound team a shiny object after three or four months and one sale. It took 12 months to fix a 100-step chain and reached half of revenue, which carried the company through COVID gym closures. Lesson: judge a long build by milestones fixed, not binary results. (S049, S052, S077, S112)

10. **Selling Gym Launch for $46.2M.** After taking about $40M in distributions he sold Gym Launch and Prestige Labs, nearly refused over one acquaintance's opinion, and saw the buyer's team was better than his. Lesson: name the voice you fear, and build a vision big enough to attract better talent. (S018, S028, S034, S044, S047, S051, S058, S062)

11. **ALAN, the $300M miss.** He built lead-working software instead of a CRM and calls it his biggest strategic miss. Lesson: capital allocated to the wrong product is the expensive mistake. (S037, S046, S074, S078, S103)

12. **$100M Offers, posted and ghosted.** He sold the book for 99 cents with a free course, did no paid marketing, and it kept selling for years on word of mouth. Lesson: a great product compounds in a way marketing cannot match. (S001, S088, S112, S113, S119, S120)

13. **The $100M Leads launch.** He tested titles, rehearsed the presentation about 100 times, and launched to about 463,000 registrations with about 10,000 free affiliates. On Vegas walks, 19 of 20 people had never heard of it. Lesson: reps create "natural talent," and you are far less known than you think. (S042, S048, S052, S084, S085, S112, S119, S120)

14. **The $100M Money Models launch.** Pre-orders using money-model mechanics funded the ads, ending at 2,800 ads and $500K a day in spend; he reports $105M to $106M in 72 hours and a Guinness record. Lesson: prove the model at small spend, then recycle the cash at volume. (S012, S020, S021, S022, S034, S050, S075, S100, S101, S109, S113)

15. **Portfolio turnarounds from price and offer.** A weight-loss brand raised prices 50% and went from $450K to $1M a month in eight weeks; an agency offer went from 0.5:1 to 11.2:1; a SaaS cut cancellations 61%. Lesson: price, offer, sales process, and retention beat more leads. (S017, S018, S021, S069, S082, S113, S114)

16. **Scale or Fail clients.** A roofer went from about $30K to about $247K a month in 90 days; a dental client went from about $160K to nearly $500K a month; a railings company grew profit from $384K to $540K. Lesson: correct advice, executed ruthlessly, compresses years into months. (S061, S073, S090, S092, S094, S097, S098, S100, S101, S106, S111)

17. **The sales-floor reps.** A solar rep who called every new booking at once sold about 4x the next rep; a timeshare rep got the best leads routed to the best closers and 5x'd his income. Lesson: what happens outside the call beats script tweaks. (S007, S009, S033, S069, S074, S087, S124)

18. **Views versus revenue.** Entertainment-style content experiments doubled views and halved ad revenue or cut book sales, while a dietitian with 5,800 followers made about $1M a year posting only about insurance billing. Lesson: make content for the highest-value buyer and judge it by revenue. (S041, S049, S054, S070, S091, S121)
