# Books: Arianna Huffington

Built only from how she teaches the books in public (book tours, keynotes, and interviews in the corpus). No book text is reproduced. She has written 15 books by her own count (S062); the three that matter for this seat are Thrive, The Sleep Revolution, and Your Time to Thrive.

## Thrive: The Third Metric to Redefining Success and Creating a Life of Well-Being, Wisdom, and Wonder (March 2014)

- **Year:** Spring 2014; Sheryl Sandberg interviewed her on the launch tour (S008), and Talks at Google said it "came out two days ago" (S065).
- **Origin:** Her 2013 Smith College commencement speech, which introduced the Third Metric, turned into the book; she calls it a "surprise baby" after promising not to write a 14th book (S010).
- **Core thesis:** Success defined only by money and power is a two-legged stool. Add a third metric with four pillars, well-being, wisdom, wonder, and giving, and you will be more effective, not less.
- **Key frameworks:** The Third Metric and four pillars; the eulogy versus resume test; "complete a project by dropping it"; 12 "microscopic steps," three at the end of each section, deliberately 12 because we are addicted to burnout (S009, S011, S050); Thrive buddies for accountability. See frameworks.md #1, #2, #14.
- **Taught in:** S005 (precursor keynote), S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S011, S012, S013, S014, S045, S050, S065.
- **Figures she cites:** 55 pages of scientific endnotes (S006, S065); an Oprah e-course built on it with Kobe Bryant as a guest teacher (S014). In 2014 she said that if she rewrote it, she would add that "The current definition of success is literally killing us" (S008).

## The Sleep Revolution: Transforming Your Life, One Night at a Time (April 2016)

- **Year:** April 2016, "nine years ago almost to the day" after her collapse (S016, S019); her 15th book, which she vowed would be her last (S026).
- **Core thesis:** Sleep deprivation is a culture-wide crisis rooted in the Industrial Revolution habit of treating humans like machines. The science is now conclusive: sleep is a performance enhancer, not a luxury, and "sleep deprivation is the new smoking."
- **Structure:** First half the why (history, science), second half the how, on purpose, because "it doesn't matter how much I tell you what to do if I haven't convinced you" (S020). A 12-point sleep manifesto; 12 sleep meditations in the appendix (S034).
- **Key frameworks:** The transition-to-sleep ritual with devices out of the bedroom as the non-negotiable step; sleep as the first leg of the health stool with food and exercise; "come for the performance-enhancing benefits, stay for the life-enhancing benefits" (S023). See frameworks.md #11 and #12.
- **Taught in:** S016, S017, S018, S019, S020, S021, S023, S026, S034.
- **Figures she cites:** About 1,200 footnotes (S023); first sleep center at Stanford in 1970, now 2,500+ (S019, S021); drowsy driving at about 8,000 deaths and 1.2M crashes a year (S016, S017); a sleep tour to 100 to 350 colleges, depending on the telling (S017, S026).

## Your Time to Thrive: End Burnout, Increase Well-being, and Unlock Your Full Potential with the New Science of Microsteps (March 2021, with Marina Khidekel and the Thrive Global team)

- **Year:** Launched March 2021, mid-pandemic (S044).
- **Core thesis:** People are already overwhelmed, so advice like "sleep eight hours" or "meditate 20 minutes" becomes one more thing to stress about. Break change into microsteps "too small to fail," science-backed and judgment-free.
- **Structure:** Eight chapters she calls pillars, sleep first; each chapter includes a Thrive staffer's microstep diary (S044).
- **Key frameworks:** Microsteps; the 60-second Reset and cumulative stress; declare an end to your workday; "no wrong way to do microsteps." See frameworks.md #10, #12, #15.
- **Taught in:** S041, S042, S043, S044.

## Earlier books (context only)

- **The Female Woman (1973):** Written at 23 after a publisher saw her televised Cambridge Union debate and she replied "I can't write" (S028, S041). It argued for equal respect for women who choose careers and women who choose full-time motherhood, which was controversial then (S028).
- **After Reason (the much-rejected second book):** On the crisis of political leadership; rejected 36 or 37 times (27 plus 13 in one telling) before a publisher said yes (S011, S033, S047). She now says it was "academic in a bad way" (S047).
- **Biographies of Maria Callas and Picasso:** The Callas book was a bestseller that eased her move to New York (S028, S035).
- **The Fourth Instinct (1994):** Beyond survival, sex, and power, an instinct toward meaning; she jokes that "about four people read" it (S041, S055).
- **On Becoming Fearless (2006):** Taught in the oldest source, S001: fearlessness as the mastery of fear, not its absence; the "obnoxious roommate."

## Related, not books

- **Goodnight Smartphone:** an Audible parody of Goodnight Moon she uses in talks about bedtime rituals (S029, S038, S049).
- **Thrive Global microstep library and Thrive Reset:** 700+ microsteps by 2018 (S037), thousands by 2024 (S053); the Reset launched in 2020 (S036).
