# Sources: Arianna Huffington

66 sources, all video transcripts (YouTube auto captions), about 400,940 words, 2007 to 2026. The mix: book talks and keynotes for Thrive (2014) and The Sleep Revolution (2016), including four Talks at Google sessions, Harvard T.H. Chan, Chicago Humanities, UChicago Institute of Politics, Stanford GSB and Stanford Medicine; five commencement and class day speeches (Smith 2013, University of Santa Monica 2015, Fletcher 2016, Sarah Lawrence, USC Baccalaureate 2025); her TED talk (2010, posted 2011); long-form podcasts where she is the guest (Tim Ferriss, Diary of a CEO, Masters of Scale, Jay Shetty x2, Rangan Chatterjee, Mark Hyman, Lewis Howes x2, 10% Happier, Chase Jarvis, Lex Fridman, Female Founders Fund, Baratunde Thurston, Nicholas Thompson); business and leadership stages (HubSpot INBOUND 2013, LinkedIn Talent Connect 2014, TechCrunch Live with Kleiner Perkins, Masters of Scale Summit, Gallup, PayPal, Valence, CECP, NAWBO); TV and news long-form (CNBC, ABC, Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, Forbes); and the Thrive AI Health and AI era talks (2024 to 2026). The oldest source (S001, Talks at Google, June 2007, the On Becoming Fearless tour) comes two months after her collapse and before Thrive, so it shows her earlier, fear-focused framing.

Exclusions and caveats: the Diary of a CEO episode with Steven Bartlett (S064, recorded around mid 2024) is a full re-upload on the "Beyond The Mic" channel, because the official full episode was not available; two official Diary of a CEO clip videos were dropped as duplicates of it. A re-upload of the Tim Ferriss episode (BlackNova Productions) was dropped as a duplicate of S028. An AI-narrated "Startups" channel video and a DLD panel where she mostly interviews Josh Kushner were dropped because she is not the main speaker. S008 is Sheryl Sandberg interviewing her (Huffington speaks most); S052 (with Padmasree Warrior) and S053 (with Deepika Padukone) are shared stages. S031 (Valuetainment) mixes host narration with interview. A Nicholas Thompson video where she is the interviewer, not the subject, was dropped. Other caveats: S025 (CNBC) begins with an unrelated cybersecurity talk that makes up more than half of its word count, and only the Andrew Ross Sorkin interview with her is used; S049 (Masters of Scale) is narrated mostly by Reid Hoffman with her clips; S044, S048 and S052 are shared stages where she has 30 to 40% of the words. Several uploads are re-posts of older footage: S026 (recorded 2016), S037 (c. 2018), S049 (c. 2018), S050 (a 2014 Thrive tour interview posted in 2024), S052 (a 2022 summit), S065 (2014), S066 (2011). No book text is used: the books are covered only through her own public teaching of them.

Dates are YouTube publish dates, which can lag the event (the TED talk was given in December 2010; several 2014 Thrive talks were posted the same spring). Blank dates were not available. Quotes come from auto captions, so punctuation was added during extraction and caption mis-hearings ("Ariana", "Hington Post") are kept as captured.
