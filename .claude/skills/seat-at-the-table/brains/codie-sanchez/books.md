# Books: Codie Sanchez

Built only from how she teaches the books in public (her own chapter walkthrough, launch interviews, and talks in the corpus). No book text is reproduced.

## Main Street Millionaire (December 2024)

- **Year:** Launched in December 2024; she walks through it chapter by chapter ahead of a December 12 launch event (S041), and the launch-era interviews run through December 2024 (S035, S036, S037, S038, S040, S042, S043).
- **Core thesis:** Ownership is the strongest predictor of wealth, and it has been taken over by private equity and large corporations. Ordinary people can buy profitable, boring, local businesses from retiring owners using other people's money, mostly seller financing, and run them through an operator. "If we don't buy back our local businesses if we don't get ownership and skin in the game then we become this nation of surfs" (S041).
- **Structure as she teaches it:** RICH, four steps across about 10 steps and 26 or more tools (S041):
  - **Research:** the cool-but-not-profitable matrix, the Lindy effect, the 3-9-12 timeline (3 months learning, 9 to find and close, 12 to stabilize), the 100-50-10-1 funnel, the 7 Ds of why owners sell (divorce, death, disease, distress, dullness, departure, disagreement), businesses she hates (dry cleaners among them), the Perfect Fit Venn, deal box and deal clarity worksheet (S041, S042).
  - **Invest:** sourcing (12 or more ways, the "4 As" of accountants, attorneys, insurance agents, advisors), seller financing as "profit paybacks," 21 creative financing methods, good versus bad debt, the deal team, 12 partnership rules, negotiation borrowed from Chris Voss and Evy Poumpouras (S035, S041, S042).
  - **Command:** hire an operator, pay generously, a takeover timeline (days 0 to 60 transition, 60 to 90 stabilize, 90 to 120 power up), Financial Fridays, cash suck versus cash flow, CADO (cut, automate, delegate, outsource) (S041).
  - **Harness:** grow from $100K to $1M revenue in seven steps, three prices plus a recurring option, the seven acquisitions inside one business, the Cash Out Cake for a premium exit (S041).
- **Four levels of business in the book:** solo venture (gateway businesses), hands-on CEO, on-deck operator, market leader (S035).
- **Figures she cites:** 22,000 pre-orders (S043); about 30,000 copies sold by late December 2024 (S044); she says she makes 10 to 15% royalty, "a couple bucks per book" (S051). A whole chapter covers her own mistakes, including the $12M fraud (S041).

## Own or Be Owned (September 2026)

- **Year:** Launched in September 2026; she promotes a September 18 launch (S061) and teaches it in launch interviews (S064, S065).
- **Core thesis:** Main Street Millionaire was "go buy"; this one is "run it profitably." Most of the roughly 15,000 owners her company has worked with are owned by their businesses: no exit, thin or no profit, everything depends on them (S064).
- **Key frameworks:** an owner score with 12 owner archetypes (most common: closer, ball hog, visionary); the 12 profit levers scored against a data set of about 30,000 businesses, starting with price, persona, and a process for profits; 90-day review loops; lifestyle, scale, or exit as the one mode per year; the pricing ladder up to value-based pricing at 10 to 30% of value delivered; PE "top five" systems (S062, S064, S065).
- **Taught in:** S062, S064, S065, and pieces in S061 and S063.
- **Figures she cites:** of about 1,600 businesses analyzed, none were overpriced (S065); artists and founders price 30 to 40% below closers (S064).
