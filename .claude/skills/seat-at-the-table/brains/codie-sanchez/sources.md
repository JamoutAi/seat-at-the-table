# Sources: Codie Sanchez

66 sources, all video transcripts (YouTube auto captions), about 853,000 words, 2020 to 2026. 27 are from her own channels (Codie Sanchez and BigDeal by Codie Sanchez): solo teaching videos on buying businesses, seller financing, debt, pricing, sales, income streams, and selling a business, the 80-minute deal sourcing playbook, her Main Street Millionaire chapter walkthrough, and live keynotes posted there. 39 are long-form interviews and stages where she is the main guest: Diary of a CEO (x2), My First Million (x2), Impact Theory with Tom Bilyeu (x3), Modern Wisdom (x2), Lewis Howes (x3, one a keynote at his Summit of Greatness), Young and Profiting (x2), The Iced Coffee Hour (x2), BiggerPockets (x3), Jay Shetty, Afford Anything, Foundr, Andrew Wilkinson, Anthony Pompliano, Ali Abdaal, Alex Lieberman, Greg Isenberg, Eric Jorgenson, Mike Rowe, Motley Fool, Rapid Response with Bob Safian, Simon Squibb, Sean Kim, Hot Smart Rich, Neville Medhora, Jamil Damji, Real Estate Rockstars, a GaryVee conversation, and a Female Startup Club live stage. No book text is used: Main Street Millionaire (2024) and Own or Be Owned (2026) are covered only through her own public teaching of them.

Exclusions and caveats. Three fetched videos were dropped as re-uploads after an overlap check: a September 2024 Tom Bilyeu episode that is about 80% stitched from S017 and S025, an April 2026 Young and Profiting "classic" that repeats S035, and a September 2026 Jay Shetty episode that is a re-cut of S050. Several interviews are shared conversations where the host talks at length (S005 Greg Isenberg with Sahil Bloom, S018 and S062 Chris Williamson, S019 My First Million with Shaan Puri, S025 and S038 Tom Bilyeu, S045 Gary Vaynerchuk, who speaks more than she does); only her words were used. S027 is a field interview where the trash company owner speaks about 70% of the words, and S031 is a case study where the buyer tells most of the deal. Several of her own videos, including S049, S051, S057, S058, and S059, carry sponsor reads and promotion of businesses she owns (BizScout, Contrarian Community, Boardroom, MSM Live); treat those segments as commercial, not analysis. Her own figures drift between tellings (first laundromat: bought for about $100K with about $67K profit, sometimes described as revenue; portfolio size from 24 to 30 businesses and $50M to nine figures), so treat numbers as she tells them. Dates are YouTube publish dates; S066's date was read from the watch page. Quotes come from auto captions, so punctuation was added during extraction and caption mishearings ("Cody") are kept as captured.
