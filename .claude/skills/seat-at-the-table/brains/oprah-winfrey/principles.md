# Principles: Oprah Winfrey

26 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from captions.

## Purpose and intention

**1. Decide the intention before the action. The intention determines the result.**
- "Do not bring me a show, unless you have fully thought out what is your intention for doing it." (S001)
- "that's why we were number one for 25 years because our programming was intentionally conceived and designed to do exactly what we did" (S010)
- Sources: 14 (S001, S003, S010, S011, S012, S018, S021, S022, S023, S026, S027, S048, S057, S058). Confidence: high.

**2. Know the mission is bigger than the product, and say it out loud from day one.**
- "the mission of the show was actually bigger than the show itself" (S010)
- "I'm trying to bring little pieces of light into people's lives." (S001)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S010, S012, S021, S027, S038). Confidence: high.

**3. Use the platform; never let it use you or humiliate the people on it.**
- "I will never be used for somebody else's agenda again" (S010)
- "I will not allow myself to have a platform where someone can be humiliated or used in that way." (S027)
- Sources: 11 (S001, S002, S004, S006, S010, S011, S012, S018, S021, S026, S027). Confidence: high.

**4. Serve first; the work becomes an offering instead of a job.**
- "if you shift the paradigm to how do I use this in service to the people who are coming and wanting your product or wanting your service" (S021)
- "when you choose the paradigm of service, looking at life through that paradigm, it turns everything you do from a job into a gift." (S004)
- Sources: 8 (S004, S006, S010, S012, S018, S021, S023, S060). Confidence: high.

**5. Know why you want it bigger before you make it bigger.**
- "And if you make the decision that you're going to make it bigger and grander, you also have to make that decision out of why you want it to be bigger." (S058)
- "I asked myself what do I really want to say to the world what is the real reason why I created in that work" (S038)
- Sources: 3 (S038, S058, S022). Confidence: high.

## Audience and trust

- Sources: 8 (S002, S004, S008, S020, S022, S026, S040, S042). Confidence: high.

**10. Excellence is the brand, and the right thing when nobody is looking compounds.**

- Sources: 3 (S005, S006, S042). Confidence: high.

**11. Protect the people your product touches, even the ones not in the room.**

## Ownership and money

**12. Own yourself. Trade a guaranteed salary for ownership when you believe in the product.**
- "I believed that ownership was the way for me." (S022)
- "So, I will take less now and in order to get more later." (S021)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S007, S014, S021, S022, S040). Confidence: high.

**13. Negotiate to share in what you create, not just to be paid for your labor.**
- "walking into boardrooms in the '80s negotiating deals to OWN my own show, not just do the show, but to make as much money from it as they were going to make off of me." (S007)
- "If they thought it was that successful, they would have never given me 50%." (S022)
- Sources: 3 (S007, S021, S022). Confidence: high.

**14. Know your numbers personally. Sign the checks.**

- "sign every check, I still do" (S057)
- Sources: 2 (S040, S057). Confidence: medium.

**15. Money matters; meaning is the point; define enough.**
- "my focus has never ever for one minute been money and the fact that the money has come has really surprised me" (S040)
- "you reach a point where you have to ask yourself what is enough what is enough" (S018)
- Sources: 7 (S004, S005, S010, S018, S025, S033, S040). Confidence: high.

**16. Use your leverage for the people below you.**
- "either they're going to get raises or I'm going to sit down." (S014)
- "it takes a while to develop a voice, but once you have it, you damn sure better use it on stuff that matters." (S014)
- Sources: 3 (S012, S014, S011). Confidence: high.

## Deciding

**17. If it doesn't feel right, don't do it. The gut is data.**
- "If it doesn't feel right, don't do it. That's the lesson." (S004)
- "every decision that I have made that has been profitable for me, that has been successful for me" (S027)
- Sources: 13 (S001, S002, S004, S005, S007, S010, S021, S023, S025, S027, S037, S040, S056). Confidence: high.

**18. When you don't know, wait. A deadline you announced is not a reason.**
- "when you don't know what to do I've learned do nothing until you do" (S010)
- "the top five was launching when we really weren't ready to launch and doing that because you'd announced that you were going to do it" (S032)
- Sources: 6 (S004, S010, S032, S040, S055, S056). Confidence: high.

**19. Don't leap from one big thing straight into the next. Take time between chapters.**
- "the big mistake that I made was that I did not wait. I did not take the time to wait a year and figure it out." (S055)
- "my deepest deepest regret is that I didn't take the time when I ended the Oprah show before I started the next thing" (S056)
- Sources: 4 (S021, S023, S055, S056). Confidence: high.

**20. You can change your mind, even after you signed.**
- "I was keeping my word because I was trying to please the people on the other side." (S023)
- "you always have the right to change your mind" (S023)
- Sources: 2 (S023, S032). Confidence: medium.

**21. Failure is direction, not identity. Learn the lesson so you don't repeat the class.**

- Sources: 10 (S001, S002, S004, S008, S009, S010, S013, S023, S032, S038). Confidence: high.

**22. In a crisis, feel it briefly, then take the next right move.**

- "You don't have to know all the right moves. You just need to know the next one." (S007)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S004, S007, S010, S055, S023). Confidence: high.

**23. Run your own race; you are your own competition.**

- "you run your own race better than anybody" (S020)
- Sources: 6 (S002, S003, S011, S012, S020, S026). Confidence: high.
