# Sources: Oprah Winfrey

60 sources, all video transcripts (YouTube captions), about 405,457 words, spanning 1986 to 2026 (upload dates run 2007 to 2026; several are archive uploads of older footage, such as the 1986 60 Minutes profile, the 1988 Barbara Walters interview, and a 1991 long interview). The mix: 9 commencement speeches and university lectures (Stanford GSB 2014, Harvard 2013, Stanford 2008 and 2015, USC, Spelman, TSU, Wellesley, Howard), 8 keynotes and live tour talks (including the QuickBooks Connect 2015 keynote on brand and business), 2 public book conversations with Arthur Brooks (Harvard Business School, 92nd Street Y), about 25 interviews where she is the subject (T.D. Jakes, Keke Palmer, Mel Robbins, Jay Shetty, Dr. Phil, NPR Wild Card, CBS, Larry King, David Rubenstein, Google Zeitgeist, Vanity Fair), and 15 episodes of her own shows and podcasts. Six of those podcast episodes (S055 to S060) are conversations with a guest author or business leader; they are used only for lines she speaks herself.

Dropped from the raw set: duplicates (a second cut of the CBS Brooks interview), a Harvard Business School case podcast where a professor talks about her, and episodes where someone else is plainly the main speaker (her interviews of Bob Iger, Michelle Obama, Gayle King, Gary Zukav, a commentator's critique of OWN, an Ellen episode, and two guest-led podcasts). No book text: her books are covered only through how she talks about them in public.

Dates are YouTube publish dates; blank where the upload had none. Quotes come from captions, so punctuation was added during extraction. Speaker turns are not marked in auto captions; in interviews and podcasts only lines clearly hers are quoted.
