# Books: Oprah Winfrey

Built only from how she talks about the books in public (talks, interviews, and her own shows in the corpus). No book text is reproduced. Her books are not business manuals; they are collections of the life principles she applies to business. The business ideas in this brain come mostly from her talks and interviews, so this file is short on purpose.

## What I Know for Sure (2014)

- **Year:** 2014; a 10th anniversary edition is discussed in 2026 (S023).
- **Core thesis:** Short essays from her O magazine column, each built around one thing she is sure of: gratitude, resilience, connection, clarity, power.
- **Business-relevant ideas:** The decision question she reads from it with Mel Robbins: what would you do if you weren't afraid of making a mistake, being rejected, looking foolish, or being alone (S023). The phrase "what I know for sure" runs through 9 sources as her way of marking a settled belief.
- **Taught in:** S003 (mentioned as her latest book at Stanford 2015), S023.

## The Path Made Clear: Discovering Your Life's Direction and Purpose (2019)

- **Year:** 2019 (S034, S035, S046; S025 is a 2024 upload of a 2019 conversation).
- **Core thesis:** Everyone has a calling; your real job is to figure out what it is and get about the business of doing it. Built from aha moments of guests on her shows (Life Class, Master Class, Super Soul).
- **Key frameworks:** What do I really want (S034, S035, S025); the whisper before the brick; get still; the voices of the world drown out the still small voice (S025). See frameworks.md #4, #13, #14, #15.
- **Taught in:** S025, S034, S035, S046.

## What Happened to You? (2021, with Dr. Bruce Perry)

- **Year:** 2021.
- **Core thesis:** Shift from "what's wrong with you" to "what happened to you" when trying to understand behavior.
- **Business-relevant ideas:** Her own account of why confrontation in the boardroom gave her so much anxiety even when she ran Harpo: childhood whippings made her a lifelong people pleaser (S024). This is the root of her "say no" principle and her admitted blind spot.
- **Taught in:** S024, S051.

## Build the Life You Want (2023, with Arthur C. Brooks)

- **Year:** September 2023 (S018, S030, S031).
- **Core thesis:** Happiness is a direction, not a destination; you can manage emotions through metacognition; build four "accounts" of faith, family, friendship, and work that serves.
- **Business-relevant ideas:** Her story of detaching from outcomes after *Beloved* flopped (S018); the decision to change the show's intention in its second year as the turning point for both joy and ratings (S018); what is enough (S018).
- **Taught in:** S018, S019, S030, S031, S050, S051, S052, S053.

## Enough (2026, with Dr. Ania Jastreboff)

- **Year:** 2026 (S026, S028, S029).
- **Core thesis:** Obesity is a disease with a biological set point, not a failure of willpower; she describes being free of "food noise" on GLP-1 medication.
- **Business-relevant ideas:** A public change of mind. She long resisted the drugs because she had been "the poster child" for willpower and was "so motivated by shame" (S029). She frames her audience's long trust through being honest about her weight struggle (S026).
- **Taught in:** S026, S028, S029.

## Books she cites as shaping her decisions

- **The Seat of the Soul (Gary Zukav, 1989):** the source of her principle of intention and "authentic power," which she says changed how she ran the show and her business (S001, S010, S011, S012, S021, S023).
- **A New Earth (Eckhart Tolle) and A Whole New Mind (Daniel Pink):** handed out at Stanford 2008 as books that confirmed her approach (S004).
- **Conscious Capitalism (John Mackey):** cited for the idea that the people you serve matter as much as your suppliers and shareholders (S001).
