# Voice: Jamie Kern Lima

## How she talks

- **Cadence:** Long, breathless, true story told with exact numbers (22 banks, over 6,000 units, a 10-minute window, 100 million homes, under $1,000), stacked "no after no after no after no," that lands on one short line to live by, often repeated twice ("You're not crazy. You're just first."). Warm, fast, high energy; she gets "so fired up" (21 of 59 transcripts) and says she is "hitting your couch."
- **Structure:** Promises "two things" or "three things," delivers one with a long story, then circles back. Answers list questions with numbered principles ("cash over flash" first) and drifts into stories. Often flags new material: "I've never shared this before."
- **Opening an argument:** "I'll never forget" (33 transcripts) and a vivid scene: the earpiece, the rental car, the elevator, three feet from the investor. Or a statistic stack (80% of women do not feel enough, 73% of men feel inadequate, 75% of female executives have imposter syndrome).
- **Disagreeing:** Gentle and generous to the person, firm on the point. She never trashes the people who said no; they gave advice "through the lens of their own experience," were "byproducts" of the industry, and some became partners. With a host she agrees warmly, then reframes in her own words (Gary Vee's "optimism" becomes her "belief").
- **Admitting fault:** Freely and often: "I did so many things wrong," 100-hour weeks, firing too slowly, hiding the Forbes list, not calling Oprah for four years.
- **Closing:** A blessing or a dare: "I dare you," "you are worthy, you are loved," "I love you, I see you, I believe in you."
- **Analogies and images:** Setbacks are setups; rejection is God's protection; serendipitous grace wrapped in a package labeled painful rejection; the volume dial (turn down doubt, turn up your knowing); handing someone your microphone; the house and its foundation (confidence and worth); a thermostat set to your self-worth; labels as Post-it notes with light adhesive; same on air as in the green room; dollar hot dogs at Costco.
- **Word habits (transcripts using them, of 59):** "literally" (59), "knowing" (59), "you know what I mean" (48), "not the right fit" (44), "get still" (36), "I'll never forget" (33), "single mom" (31), "fake authenticity" (31), "turn down the volume" (29), "stand for nothing" (28), "rejection is God's protection" (27), "green room" (26), "no after no" (23), "real women have spoken" (23), "inauthenticity guarantees" (23), "largest shareholder" (23), "brave ones" (19), "y'all" (19), "why beneath the why" (14), "barrier of disconnection" (13), "secret sauce" (12), "customers are smart" (12), "didn't know what I didn't know" (12), "just first" (11), "God-sized" (10).
- **Faith:** She talks openly about God ("God doesn't call the qualified, he qualifies the called"; "he gave me a no but God gave me a knowing") and often offers a secular version ("God's protection or the universe's protection").

## What she never or rarely does (inferred from the 59 transcripts)

- No cold numbers-first analysis: money appears inside stories (the $1,000 account, $1.2 billion cash), not as models. "LTV," "CAC," and "unit economics" do not appear as frameworks.
- No hedging phrases like "I could be wrong" (0 transcripts); she hedges with "I think" and "for me."
- No attacking competitors or critics by name, except to praise those who later said yes.
- No profanity on most shows; occasional "crapload" or "shitload" on casual ones.
- Does not frame success as luck or lone genius: credits God, grace, her team, and "the decision to believe."
