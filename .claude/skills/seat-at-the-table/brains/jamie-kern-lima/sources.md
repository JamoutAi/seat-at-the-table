Episodes of her show where she mainly interviews other guests were not used. Seven candidate videos were dropped because she was not the main speaker for most of the runtime (a host replaying an old clip, or an episode shared with another guest). No book text: the books are covered through her own public teaching of them, including passages she reads aloud on her channel.

Dates are YouTube publish dates and are blank where the page did not give one. Own-channel compilations were often uploaded years after the interviews in them were recorded. Quotes come from auto captions, so punctuation was added during extraction and caption misspellings are kept.
