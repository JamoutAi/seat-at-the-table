## Customer and product

- "Maybe people are sick and tired of not seeing anyone that looks like them um and and products that don't work for them." (S011)
- Sources: 20+ (S007, S011, S015, S018, S036, S047, S031, S003 and most QVC tellings). Confidence: high.

**2. Customers buy the feeling of being seen, not just the fix. Your market is bigger than your origin problem.**
- "5% of our customers actually have skin issues like I do. 95% don't." (S015)

- Sources: 5 (S015, S050, S038, S017, S029). Confidence: high.

**3. Run every product and channel decision through one question: does this serve her?**

- "I would throw your ego out the window. I'd be ready to be super scrappy." (S014)
- Sources: 6 (S041, S033, S003, S014, S010, S042). Confidence: high.

**4. Protect customer trust over a short-term sales spike. The trust is the asset.**

- Sources: 6 (S010, S033, S003, S014, S023, S041). Confidence: high.

**5. A great product does not sell itself. Plan for awareness and distribution from day one.**

- Sources: 12 (S013, S031, S006, S004, S021, S035, S012, S010, S025, S047, S009, S038). Confidence: high.

**6. Get operations right even when they are not your passion.**
- "Oh, if you're operations aren't right, it can impact everything else." (S015)

- Sources: 4 (S015, S026, S035, S008). Confidence: high.

**7. Being first is not the same as being right on timing.**
- "Sometimes timing, uh, you know, just being first isn't always isn't always optimal." (S020)

## Rejection and conviction

**13. Never take a business no personally. Treat it as a future yes on your terms.**
- "Professionally, I always made sure to not take it personally." (S019)
- "I am going to in every ounce of my being respond to that rejection as if I am fully convinced it will be a yes." (S028)
- Sources: 15 (S019, S028, S042, S053, S031, S002, S012, S016, S050, S026, S038, S023, S004, S035, S030). Confidence: high.

**14. Rejection is not a market signal on its own. Decode what the no actually says.**
- "When a retailer says no to you, what they're saying is I don't think that we can make money from you." (S015)
- "Rejection is almost never personal and it's almost never an indication that your idea or your dream isn't going to work." (S030)
- Sources: 10 (S015, S030, S007, S018, S047, S024, S059, S017, S052, S005). Confidence: high.

**15. Experts judge new ideas by what has already worked. Expect them to say no to anything authentic.**

- Sources: 18. Confidence: high.

**16. Weigh advice by whether the advisor has built something. Then check it against your gut.**

**17. Your knowing beats anyone's advice, including your best mentor's. Most of her mistakes came from ignoring it.**

- "I believe your intuition is more powerful than anyone else's advice, even your greatest mentor." (S025)

**18. The victory is hearing your truth, not refusing to quit. Sometimes the right call is to let go.**
