# Books: Jamie Kern Lima

Built only from how she teaches the books in public (launch interviews, her own podcast, and solo episodes where she reads passages aloud). No book text is reproduced beyond short lines she says on camera. She wrote both herself, turned down ghostwriters and book deals for years ("I didn't want to have a ghostriter do it. So I said no for five years," S024), and donates 100% of her author proceeds (Feeding America, Together Rising, and programs building self-worth in girls and women).

## Believe IT: How to Go from Underestimated to Unstoppable (February 2021)

- **Year:** Launched in February 2021; much of the corpus is its launch tour (sources dated 2021-02 to 2021-08).
- **Core thesis:** Her story, told for the first time with "the stories behind the stories": not a Denny's waitress turned billionaire fairy tale, but "a girl who went from not believing in herself to believing herself." The real skill was learning to hear and trust her own intuition over the noise of experts, rejection, and self-doubt.
- **Key frameworks:** Trusting your gut over expert advice (frameworks.md #13); knowing when to let go of a dream as much as when to go after one; the why beneath the why (#6); never taking rejection personally (#10); the volume dial and the microphone (who gets to speak into your dreams); setbacks are setups; "God doesn't call the qualified, he qualifies the called." Chapter 1 is about trusting intuition: "when your intuition talks to you believe it" (S050).
- **Taught in:** S002, S004, S003, S009, S027, S026, S011, S031, S042, S053, S050, S056, S054, S034, S012.
- **Figures she cites:** About 95% of the stories never shared before (S012); nine weeks on the New York Times list (S058); 2.2 million meals raised in launch week (S013).
- **Her own description:** "Believe IT was my journey," and Worthy is "the playbook" (S046).

## Worthy: How to Believe You Are Enough and Transform Your Life (February 2024)

- **Year:** Announced in June 2023 (S037), published February 2024.
- **Core thesis:** Self-confidence and self-worth are different, and self-worth is "the one thing that changes everything." "We don't rise to what we believe is possible, we fall to what we believe we're worthy of." Achievement builds confidence but never worth, so high achievers stay stuck, sabotage, hit ceilings, or feel empty. Building worth is unlearning lies, and it makes you more ambitious, not less.
- **Structure as she walks it (S049):** Part 1 Seeing (ch. 1 the one thing that changes everything; ch. 2 change your relationship with rejection, the Four Rs, the longest chapter and originally its own book; ch. 3 you're not crazy, you're just first; ch. 4 you have greatness inside of you). Part 2 Unlearning the lies (don't wait on your weight; I should only be seen when I'm happy; I don't deserve better; I don't have anything special to offer; I need to please them to love me; if I stand out I'll get kicked out; I'm an impostor; if I'm me I won't be loved; labels are permanent). Part 3 Transforming (self-worth is the multiplier; do you see you; know your why then fly girl fly; circle or cage; overexposed and underdeveloped; transformations). Part 4 Knowing (your ticket to the moon, on intuition; who are you really doubting; solos; your victory lap). Over 20 tools.
- **Key frameworks:** Self-confidence vs self-worth and the fulfillment multiplier (frameworks.md #20); the confidence/worth chart with persons A to D (S045); the Four Rs of rejection (#12); the thermostat; hiding in plain sight; people pleasing as self-betrayal ("how does this feel to my soul?"); self-worth shortcuts.
- **Business framing she gives it:** "If you want to double your business double your selfworth." (S032) Low worth shows up as revenue ceilings, sabotage, and leaders who will not risk their novel ideas while numbers are good.
- **Taught in:** S005, S055, S057, S058, S059, S052, S030, S032, S045, S046, S051, S044, S048, S049, S039, S040, S029, S037, S047.
- **Figures she cites:** 80% of women do not believe they are enough; 73% of men feel inadequate; 75% of female executives deal with imposter syndrome; 91% of women do not love their bodies; 89% of girls and women opt out of meaningful activities when they do not like how they look; over 50% of women and over 40% of men call themselves people pleasers (S005, S045, S052, S046).

## The Jamie Kern Lima Show (podcast, 2024 onward)

- Not a book, but her teaching home since 2024: solo episodes that read Worthy chapters aloud (S045, S040, S039) and compilations of her interviews. Its first episode is her full founder story (S047).
