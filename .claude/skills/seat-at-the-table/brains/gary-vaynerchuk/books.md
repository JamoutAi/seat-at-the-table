# Gary Vaynerchuk: books (as he teaches them in public)

No book text is reproduced here. Theses and frameworks are summarized in plain words from his own talks, posts, and decks in the corpus.

## Crush It! (2009)
- **Thesis:** The internet lets an ordinary person build a personal brand and a living around a passion, with no gatekeeper; he wrote that you could make $50K a year on YouTube and was called a snake oil salesman.
- **Key ideas:** passion plus hard work plus patience; build on new platforms early; the individual as a media brand.
- **Taught in:** S018, S045, S047, S074, S153.

## The Thank You Economy (2011)
- **Thesis:** Social media returns business to small-town values: one-to-one care, gratitude, and listening win retention and word of mouth, and retention beats acquisition.
- **Key ideas:** a "thank you department" playing offense; call every new customer just to thank them; map customers' public interests for specific gestures; humans cannot be automated out of real care.
- **Taught in:** S019, S077, S047, S137.

## Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook (2013)
- **Thesis:** Give value repeatedly in platform-native, contextual content, then make a clear ask; respect each platform's context.
- **Key ideas:** jabs earn the chance to ask, not a guaranteed sale; match the hook's tone to the jabs; remove friction from the ask; social must also drive direct response.
- **Taught in:** S011, S045, S047, S122, S123, S137.

## #AskGaryVee (2016)
- **Thesis:** A book of his Q&A show answers on business, marketing, and personal questions; the show itself was a pillar content format.
- **Key ideas:** self-awareness, practical tactics, the Q&A as pillar content.
- **Taught in:** S004, S020, S022 (show episodes). No separate summary beyond the show content.

## Crushing It! (2018)
- **Thesis:** An update of Crush It! with case studies of readers who built brands; the principles hold while platform tactics change every six to nine months.
- **Key ideas:** platform-by-platform playbooks, personal brand as optionality, money plus happiness as the definition of crushing it.
- **Taught in:** S153.

## Twelve and a Half (2021)
- **Thesis:** Emotional intelligence decides business outcomes; twelve ingredients plus the "half," the weakness you name publicly (his was candor).
- **Key ideas:** gratitude, self-awareness, accountability, optimism, empathy, kindness, tenacity, curiosity, patience, conviction, humility, ambition; kind candor as his half.
- **Taught in:** S086 to S091, S146 to S152, S037, S065.

## Day Trading Attention (2024)
- **Thesis:** Attention is the asset, interest-graph platforms make creative the variable of success, and marketing must be rebuilt around organic content, cohorts, and validation before paid media.
- **Key ideas:** Modern Advertising Framework (cohorts, PAC, SOC, amplification, modern commercials, post-creative strategy, comments as creative).
- **Taught in:** S035, S096, S145, S043, S054.

## In progress: The Individual Empire (mentioned 2026)
- **Thesis as he states it:** a person's brand can now be the core of a corporation; "now we're at the end state where a human can be a corporation." [S057, 2026]
- **Taught in:** S057, S045, S074.
